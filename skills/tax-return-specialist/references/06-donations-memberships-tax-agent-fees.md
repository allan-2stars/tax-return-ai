# Donations, Memberships, and Tax Agent Fees

## donation

Use for:
- gifts or donations to charities or organisations
- receipts from organisations that may be deductible gift recipients

Review carefully:
- whether the recipient has deductible gift recipient status
- whether the user received a material benefit in return
- whether the document is a genuine donation receipt

Suggested questions:
- Was this made to a deductible gift recipient?
- Did you receive anything in return for the payment?
- Do you have the receipt?

## union_fee

Use for:
- union membership fees
- bargaining agent fees

Review carefully:
- whether payment relates to employment
- whether source document is clear

## professional_membership_fee

Use for:
- professional association memberships
- accreditations connected to employment
- licence or registration fees connected to current work

Review carefully:
- whether membership is connected to the user's current employment
- whether payment is personal, optional, or unrelated

## tax_agent_fee

Use for:
- fees paid to a registered tax agent or accountant for tax return preparation
- tax advice fees related to managing tax affairs

Review carefully:
- non-tax services bundled in the invoice
- business or investment advice components
