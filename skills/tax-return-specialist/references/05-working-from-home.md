# Working From Home

## Purpose

Use `work_from_home` for candidate expenses connected to working from home.

Potential source documents:
- internet bills
- electricity bills
- mobile phone bills
- stationery receipts
- office equipment receipts
- timesheets or home working hour records

## Classification Behaviour

Working-from-home items usually need user confirmation because they often involve mixed private/work use.

Default behaviour:
- set `review_status` to `needs_user_review` unless evidence and work-use details are clear
- require work-use percentage or work-from-home hours where relevant
- preserve source evidence

## Common Review Questions

Ask targeted questions:
- How many hours did you work from home during the relevant period?
- What percentage of this bill or item was work-related?
- Was any part reimbursed by your employer?
- Was this expense also used by other household members?
- Do you have records of work-from-home hours?

## Risk Signals

Mark medium or high risk when:
- full household bill is claimed without apportionment
- no work-from-home hours are supplied
- item is clearly mixed-use
- amount appears unusually large
- employer reimbursement may apply

## Method Handling

Do not choose a final calculation method unless the app has explicit, current rules and user-confirmed records.

The system may prepare method-ready data, but final method selection should be reviewed by the user or tax agent.
