# Work-Related Deductions

## General Test

For work-related candidate deductions, evaluate:
- the user paid or incurred the expense
- the expense has a clear connection to earning employment income
- the expense was not reimbursed
- there is evidence where required
- any private-use portion is excluded or apportioned

Do not state that the item is definitely deductible. Use candidate language.

## work_related_car_expense

Use for:
- car expenses connected to employment duties
- work travel between work sites
- work-related trips during the work day

Review carefully:
- ordinary home-to-work commuting is usually private
- car allowance does not automatically make a claim valid
- logbook or cents-per-kilometre method may require user selection and records

Suggested questions:
- Was this travel between home and your normal workplace?
- Was the trip required as part of your employment duties?
- Were you reimbursed by your employer?
- Do you have a logbook or kilometre records?

## work_related_travel_expense

Use for:
- accommodation
- flights
- taxis/rideshare
- meals while travelling overnight for work

Review carefully:
- private travel mixed with work
- employer reimbursement
- travel allowances
- family or leisure components

## work_related_clothing_laundry

Use for:
- occupation-specific clothing
- protective clothing
- compulsory uniforms
- laundry or dry-cleaning records

Review carefully:
- conventional clothing is usually private even if worn at work
- logo, protective function, or occupation-specific nature should be captured

Suggested questions:
- Is this clothing protective, occupation-specific, or a compulsory uniform?
- Is it suitable for normal everyday wear?

## work_related_self_education

Use for:
- course fees
- textbooks
- professional training
- certification costs

Review carefully:
- link to current employment duties
- new career or unrelated study
- employer reimbursement
- HELP/HECS-like payments may not be claimable and need review

## tools_equipment

Use for:
- work tools
- computer equipment
- software
- small equipment used for work

Review carefully:
- private/work split
- depreciation or immediate deduction treatment may apply depending on rules and amount
- do not invent thresholds

Suggested questions:
- What percentage is work use?
- Was this reimbursed by your employer?
- Is the item also used personally?

## income_protection

Use for:
- income protection insurance premiums

Review carefully:
- policy type
- bundled insurance products
- superannuation-paid premiums

## unknown_deduction

Use when the item may be a deduction but the category is unclear.

Set review status to `needs_user_review`.
